# Demo Scenarios

## Primary Scenario: Leaking Sink

Tenant message:

> Hi, my name is Jamie Example. The kitchen sink is leaking and water is getting into the cabinet.

Follow-up details:

- Flat 2, 14 Example Street
- Yes, it is actively dripping right now.
- Send three photos of the leak, cabinet, and wider kitchen area.
- The plumber can enter, I will be home after 2pm.

Expected triage:

- Category: Plumbing
- Urgency: High
- Vendor: RapidPipe Plumbing
- Risk: Potential water damage
- Rough price: about £260-£700
- Required evidence: 3 tenant photos

Contractor dispatch demo:

- Contact top three approved plumbers.
- RapidPipe Plumbing accepts at £520, ETA today 3:30pm.
- Northline Plumbing Co. accepts at £610, ETA today 5:00pm.
- Apex Drainage & Leak Detection declines or quotes higher.
- If landlord auto-accept is enabled with a £650 limit, RapidPipe is confirmed and the other requests are closed.
- If auto-accept is disabled, the landlord manually reviews quotes and confirms the preferred contractor.
